CS50 Letter to Your Future Self

Write a letter to your future self at the end of this semester. The beginning of a new school year is always a great time to pause and reflect on how you're feeling about this new semester!

Some questions to get you started:

  • What are you excited about?
  • What are you nervous or uncertain about?
  • What kind of support do you hope to be for students this semester?
  • How do you want to feel about your teaching come December?
